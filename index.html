[ SECTION 02 // CURRICULUM ]

Flight Academy Syllabus

Select a module below to review the standardized flight instruction criteria required for graduation.

Module 01: Basic Flight Controls & Physics

GROUND SCHOOL

Understanding rotor lift, collective pitch, cyclic management, and tail rotor compensation in Squad's unique physics engine. Cadets will learn how to maintain hover stability and execute smooth transitional lifts.

Key Learning Objectives:
  • Throttle / Collective response curves
  • Translational lift and effective vortex ring state awareness
  • Trimming and rudder control coordination
Practical Examination:

Maintain a stable 50ft hover for 3 minutes without drifting more than 5 meters in any horizontal direction.

Module 02: Precision Landing & Terrain Navigation

ADVANCED HANDLING

Real combat zones rarely have paved runways. Pilots must master touch downs on uneven forest floors, rooftops, hillsides, and tight forest clearings under time constraints.

Key Learning Objectives:
  • Steep autorotation and sliding touch-downs
  • Contour flying utilizing tree lines and hills
  • Avoiding power lines, radio towers, and urban obstacles
Practical Examination:

Land the transport helicopter on a designated mountain ridge helipad within 45 seconds of takeoff.

Module 03: Troop Transport & Logistics Drops

LOGISTICS CORPS

Logistics wins wars. This module trains pilots in maximum payload management, fuel burn calculation, and hot-dropping squads directly onto active frontlines without shutting down rotors.

Key Learning Objectives:
  • Supply crate unloads and FOB supply loops
  • Hot LZ rapid disembarkation choreography
  • Emergency troop extraction under small-arms fire
Practical Examination:

Deliver 3,000 supplies and 8 infantry troops to a contested FOB under simulated mortar fire.

Module 04: Situational Awareness & Communications

COMMAND & CONTROL

Pilots serve as the eyes in the sky. Effective radio discipline, communicating grid coordinates, calling out enemy armor positions, and coordinating with Squad Leaders (SLs) is paramount.

Key Learning Objectives:
  • Command channel radio etiquette and brevity codes
  • Map reading and compass bearing callouts
  • Threat evasion against enemy anti-air assets
Practical Examination:

Conduct a reconnaissance sweep, report 3 concealed enemy positions within 90 seconds, and return safely.

[ SECTION 03 // DIRECTIVES ]

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Strict adherence to 44th Regiment flight rules ensures asset preservation and maximum team efficiency on deployment day.

SOP_01 // CLAIMING

Asset Claiming & Squad Creation

Pilots must name their Squad explicitly as 44th Air Corps - Transport and lock squad size to prevent random infantry spawns.

SOP_02 // CRASH RECOVERY

Crash & Timer Penalties

Loss of a helicopter due to pilot negligence or reckless hot-dropping results in a mandatory 15-minute grounding penalty and review by the Flight Instructor.

SOP_03 // PRIORITY LZ

LZ Safety Hierarchy

If an LZ is marked hot by ground Squad Leaders, transport pilots must divert to a secondary LZ 300m out unless an emergency medical extraction has been authorized.
